# Known Limitations

- The live product includes in-memory demo data for a predictable public walkthrough.
- The contract demonstrates recurring billing policy; a production asset-transfer integration needs additional issuer and reconciliation controls.
- Explorer confirmation may appear after a short indexing delay.
- The documented Mainnet flow uses a limited set of demonstration actors and is not proof of unique-user adoption.
- Plan and charge identifiers are caller-managed and require a reliable allocation strategy in a scaled deployment.
- Contract upgrades are not automated in this repository.
- Notifications and scheduled keepers are application concerns and are not guaranteed by the contract alone.

These limits do not invalidate the verified Mainnet deployment or functional contract calls. They define the next engineering boundaries for production scaling.
